# Northwind Supply Co. — Shipping Policy

_Last updated: January 2026. Northwind Supply Co. is a fictional company used for demonstration._

## Shipping options and estimates

| Method | Estimated delivery | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–5 business days | Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.95 |
| Expedited | 1–2 business days | $12.95 |
| Same-day (select metro areas) | Same business day if ordered before 11 AM | $19.95 |

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed after 2 PM are processed the next business day.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed to you as soon as your order ships. You can also view tracking on the **Order History** page of your account.

## What counts as a "late" order

An order is considered late if it has **not been delivered within 10 business days** of the order date for Standard shipping, or has passed its estimated delivery window by more than 3 business days for Expedited or Same-day shipping. Late orders due to a Northwind error qualify for free return shipping and a full shipping refund.

## Lost or stuck packages

If tracking shows **no movement for 7 consecutive days**, the package is treated as potentially lost. Contact Northwind support with your order number to open a lost-package claim. We will either reship the order or issue a full refund once the claim is confirmed.

## International shipping

Northwind currently ships within the United States only. International shipping is not available.
