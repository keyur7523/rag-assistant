# Northwind Supply Co. — Warranty Policy

_Last updated: January 2026. Northwind Supply Co. is a fictional company used for demonstration._

## Coverage

All Northwind-branded electronics come with a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

Non-electronic products carry a **90-day limited warranty** against manufacturing defects.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Damage from misuse, accidents, or unauthorized modifications
- Normal wear and tear
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function
- Products with a removed or altered serial number
- Consumable parts such as batteries after 90 days

## How to file a warranty claim

1. Contact Northwind support with your order number and a description of the defect.
2. Include photos or a short video demonstrating the issue when possible.
3. Support will confirm eligibility within **2 business days** and, if approved, arrange a free repair or replacement.

## Repair vs. replacement

For approved claims, Northwind decides whether to repair or replace the item at its discretion. Replacements are shipped via Expedited shipping at no cost. If an identical replacement is unavailable, a comparable item or a full refund is offered.
