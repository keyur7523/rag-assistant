# Northwind Supply Co. — Returns Policy

_Last updated: January 2026. Northwind Supply Co. is a fictional company used for demonstration._

## Return window

You may return most items within **30 days** of the delivery date for a refund or exchange. The 30-day window is measured from the date the carrier marks your order as delivered, not the order date.

## Condition requirements

To be eligible for a return, an item must be:

- Unused and in the same condition you received it
- In its original packaging, with all tags, manuals, and accessories included
- Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt)

Items returned with visible signs of use, missing parts, or damaged packaging may be rejected or subject to a restocking fee (see the Refund Policy).

## How to start a return

1. Sign in to your account and open the **Order History** page.
2. Select the order and choose **Start a Return**.
3. Print the prepaid return label and drop the package at any authorized carrier location.

You will receive a confirmation email once the return is registered. Return shipping is free when the return is due to a Northwind error (wrong or defective item); otherwise a flat **$6.95** return-shipping fee is deducted from your refund.

## Non-returnable items

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned:

- Gift cards
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale"
- Perishable goods
- Custom or personalized products
- Downloadable software and digital products

## Exchanges

Exchanges follow the same 30-day window and condition rules as returns. If the replacement item costs more than the original, you will be charged the difference; if it costs less, the difference is refunded to your original payment method.
