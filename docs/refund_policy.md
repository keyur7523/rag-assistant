# Northwind Supply Co. — Refund Policy

_Last updated: January 2026. Northwind Supply Co. is a fictional company used for demonstration._

## Refund timing

Once your returned item arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection, refunds are processed within **5 to 7 business days**. After processing, it may take an additional 3 to 5 business days for the amount to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer.

## Refund method

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used for the purchase. If that payment method is no longer valid (for example, an expired card), the refund is issued as **store credit** instead.

Store credit is applied instantly once the return passes inspection and never expires.

## Restocking fees

Most returns are free of restocking fees. Exceptions:

- **Opened electronics** carry a **15% restocking fee**, deducted from the refund amount.
- Items returned outside the 30-day window but accepted as a courtesy carry a **20% restocking fee**.

## Shipping costs

Original shipping charges are **non-refundable**, except when the return is due to a Northwind error (wrong, damaged, or defective item), in which case shipping is fully refunded.

## Late or missing refunds

If more than **10 business days** have passed since your return passed inspection and you have not received your refund:

1. Check your bank account again.
2. Contact your card issuer, as processing times vary.
3. If you still have not received it, contact Northwind support with your order number.
